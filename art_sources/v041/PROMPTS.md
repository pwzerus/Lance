# 原始生成提示词

## 非写实鼻盔头像

Edit the reference into ONE production game head sprite on a truly transparent background. Keep the 12th–13th century conical nasal helmet, brass brow band, chainmail coif, and three-quarter face turned towards the RIGHT with chest facing camera. The key correction: the FACE MUST BE SIMPLE CHUNKY PIXEL ART, NOT REALISTIC, not a painted bearded portrait. Adult medieval rider, face represented by roughly 12 by 16 large logical pixels: one dark eye dash, a small stepped nose under the nasal guard, simple jaw and 2-tone short beard shapes, no pupils, no wrinkles, no pores, no detailed lips or realistic facial lighting. Charming rough game sprite clarity like hand-built 1990s character pixel art, not a realistic illustration with a pixel filter. Whole head and coif designed on a coherent 48 by 64 logical pixel grid enlarged with hard square edges. Limit palette to about 16 colors, 3 flat steel shades, 3 warm skin shades, dark brown outline, muted gold. Preserve readable helmet ornament and a FEW LARGE MAIL CLUSTERS, not noisy individual rings. No gradients, no antialiasing, no glossy bloom, no painterly texture. Head plus coif only, no shoulders/torso/weapons, large centered silhouette, fully contained with transparent margin. No text, no labels, no checkerboard background. This will be displayed 60–70 pixels wide in a jousting game.

## 马匹分件

Create a production TRANSPARENT modular sprite atlas for a medieval chestnut riding horse for a side scrolling jousting game. Use the supplied atlas ONLY as a palette/tack reference; REPLACE its fat pony anatomy with a lean athletic adult saddle horse with a smaller barrel and long elegant limbs. Handmade-looking chunky 16-bit PIXEL ART, crisp square-edged color clusters, dark brown outlines, about 20 warm chestnut/brown/cream/brass/red/near-black colors, 3 flat shades per material, NO painterly shading, no blur or gradients. All pieces in SIDE VIEW facing RIGHT. The assembled horse is not chibi and not a giant heavy draft horse. Plain medieval high wooden saddle with leather seat, red saddlecloth narrow gold trim, simple bridle, no plate horse armor. ATLAS LAYOUT: landscape image, FOUR equal columns and TWO equal rows, exactly EIGHT separate sprites, one per cell, generous transparent gutters, NO labels, NO grid lines. Top row left to right: (1) ONLY the horizontal horse TORSO with saddle and small saddlecloth, rump on left, shoulders on right; elegant barrel length-to-depth ratio 2.4:1; absolutely NO neck, head, tail, legs, hooves, rider, or dangling reins attached. (2) ONLY an arched horse NECK AND HEAD together with mane, small ears and plain leather bridle; lower neck root at bottom LEFT, head and muzzle facing right, long equine face, NOT cartoon smile. (3) ONLY a flowing black-brown TAIL, root at UPPER RIGHT, flowing downward and left, a few clear pixel strands. (4) ONLY one dark HOOF with short fetlock connection at top, toe points right; compact trapezoid hoof. Bottom row left to right: (1) detached FORELEG upper segment, muscular rounded shoulder root at TOP tapering to knee at BOTTOM, vertical, no hoof. (2) detached FORELEG lower cannon segment, slender straight vertical bone from top knee to bottom fetlock, NO HOOF. (3) detached HINDLEG upper thigh segment, powerful but slim, rounded hip joint at TOP tapering to hock at BOTTOM, slight curved contour, vertical, NO HOOF. (4) detached HINDLEG lower cannon segment, slender, top hock to bottom fetlock, NO HOOF. Each leg piece has simple overlapping rounded ends for skeletal animation, joint endpoints centered horizontally, and warm chestnut coat transitioning to near-black below knees. Every piece fully contained in its own cell. These are RIGGABLE CUTOUT PARTS, not a full horse and not 8 poses of a horse. Nothing else in the background, true alpha transparency, no ground or shadows.
